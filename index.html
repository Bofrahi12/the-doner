The Craft

Why we roast for 6 hours

Because the crust is not a coating — it is a structure. Fat renders, edges caramelise, and the stack tightens layer by layer until the knife meets resistance, then silk. Two to three hours gets you cooked meat. Six gets you döner.

Sourcing our veal — Anatolia to Berlin

After testing suppliers across Türkiye, Austria and Bavaria, one came out ahead on every measure: consistent marbling, clean fat, honest weights. Every stack is built by hand, every morning, in this kitchen.
